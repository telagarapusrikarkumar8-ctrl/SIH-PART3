Important Guidelines & Quality Norms (FAQ)

  • Moisture Content: Paddy must have moisture level ≤ 17.0%. Digital moisture meter testing is mandatory at gate entry.
  • Foreign Matter: Organic foreign matter must not exceed 1.0% and inorganic foreign matter ≤ 1.0%.
  • Damaged / Discoloured Grains: Maximum permissible limit is 5.0%.
  • Mandatory Documents at PPC: Farmer must bring Booking Token Printout/SMS, Original Aadhaar Card, and Bank Passbook copy.
  • Electronic Weighment: Digital weighbridge slip with Gross, Tare, and Net Weight will be handed over to farmer on the spot.
